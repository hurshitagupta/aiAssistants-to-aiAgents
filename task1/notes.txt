About AI Agents

AI agents are software systems that perceive their environment, make decisions, and take actions to achieve specific goals. 
They range from simple automation scripts to advanced autonomous assistants that learn from data and interact naturally with people. 
Modern agents combine planning, learning, and natural language understanding to handle tasks, coordinate with other agents, and adapt to changing conditions.
Responsible design and deployment require attention to safety, transparency, and ethical considerations so agents remain aligned with human values and expectations.
